Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30th of july

This Privacy Policy describes how MRC Stat Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information from your Discord account (username, Discord ID, avatar) when you log in. We also collect Roblox account data that you choose to track through our service (such as usernames, in-game inventory, and statistics).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the tracking and dashboard functionality. We do not sell your data.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare D1 (SQLite) and protected by encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as long as you maintain an account. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us via Discord.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Discord for authentication. Please review Discord’s privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please join our Discord server (link on homepage).
