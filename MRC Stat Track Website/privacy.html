Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MRC Stat Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") collects, stores, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services. By using MRC Stat Tracker, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

MRC Stat Tracker is an independent online dashboard that allows users to monitor their own Roblox account statistics, inventory, pets, and session information.

MRC Stat Tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect the following information.

Discord Account Information

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Username
  • Display Name
  • Avatar

This information is provided by Discord when you log in using Discord OAuth.

Roblox Tracking Information

When you choose to track a Roblox account, we may collect:

  • Roblox Username
  • Inventory information
  • Pet information
  • Session statistics
  • Online or Offline status

We only collect information necessary to provide the features of the Service.

Account Information

  • Your unique API key
  • Your subscription plan
  • Account creation date
  • Subscription status

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Your Discord password
  • Your Roblox password
  • Your cryptocurrency wallet's private keys
  • Your payment credentials
  • Any personal identification documents

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate and improve the Service.

Examples include:

  • Authenticating your account.
  • Displaying your dashboard.
  • Generating your personal API key.
  • Providing tracker functionality.
  • Managing subscriptions.
  • Protecting against abuse.
  • Enforcing API rate limits.
  • Improving performance and reliability.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. API Keys

Each account is assigned a unique API key.

API keys are securely stored and used only to authenticate requests made by your tracker.

You are responsible for keeping your API key private. Sharing your API key may allow another person to access your account's tracking features.

6. Cookies and Sessions

The Service may use cookies or secure session tokens to keep you logged in, maintain your authenticated session, and improve the user experience.

Disabling cookies may prevent certain features from functioning correctly.

7. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate parts of the Service. These providers may process information necessary for their services.

Examples include:

  • Discord (Authentication)
  • Cloudflare (Hosting, Workers, D1 Database, Security)
  • Cryptocurrency payment providers (for payment verification where applicable)

Your use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Storage

Your information is stored using Cloudflare infrastructure, including Cloudflare D1 databases where applicable.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Your information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the Service.

If you choose to delete or log out of your account through the Settings page, your account may be scheduled for deletion.

If you do not log back into your account within seven (7) days, your stored account information may be permanently deleted.

This process cannot normally be reversed.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information is only shared when necessary:

  • To provide the Service.
  • To comply with applicable laws.
  • To respond to valid legal requests.
  • To protect the security of the Service.
  • To investigate fraud or abuse.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including access controls, secure authentication, API authentication, and infrastructure security provided by Cloudflare.

You are also responsible for protecting your Discord account, your API key, and your devices.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your stored information.

Requests may be made through our official Discord server.

13. Children's Privacy

MRC Stat Tracker is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the Service through Discord authentication.

By using the Service, you understand that your information may be transferred accordingly.
